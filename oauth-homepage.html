What inboundSelect does

Three connected steps take a marketing lead from first ring to a confirmed appointment on your calendar.

STEP 1

Track every campaign

Connect Google Ads and Twilio to attribute each inbound call to the exact campaign, ad, and keyword that produced it. Every lead is measured, not guessed.

STEP 2

Route the lead instantly

Twilio webhooks pass each inbound call to inboundSelect, which routes it to an available, eligible team member in seconds and logs the outcome of the conversation.

STEP 3

Book it on your calendar

When a lead is ready, your team schedules the consultation and inboundSelect writes the appointment to your Google Calendar — with reminders — so it is never double-booked or forgotten.

Why inboundSelect connects to your Google Calendar

Signing in with Google is how your team links inboundSelect to the calendar where lead appointments live. We request access for one clear reason: to schedule and manage the consultations generated from your inbound marketing leads. Connecting Google Calendar is optional — the rest of the platform works without it — and you can disconnect at any time.

  • ✓Check availability — we read your calendar so we can offer open times and avoid double-booking when an appointment is set.
  • ✓Create appointments — when your team books a lead, we add the event (with the contact and notes) to your Google Calendar, including reminders.
  • ✓Keep appointments in sync — if an appointment is rescheduled or cancelled in inboundSelect, we update or remove the matching calendar event.

The specific Google scopes we request:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events

inboundSelect's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

✓We never sell your Google data and never use it for advertising.
✓We do not use your Google data to train AI or machine-learning models.
✓Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the calendar features above.
✓You can revoke access anytime in inboundSelect settings or your Google Account.
