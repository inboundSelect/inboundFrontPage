Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Inbound Select (“Inbound Select”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use our call-routing and marketing-attribution platform (the “Service”).

1. Who we are

Inbound Select is a business-to-business platform that routes inbound phone calls from marketing campaigns to licensed agents on behalf of insurance agencies, and gives agents tools to manage calls and appointments. It is intended for business users and is not directed to children.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email, phone number, role, agency affiliation, hashed password, and licensing/state-eligibility details.
  • Call and appointment data — routed-call records (caller number, US state, timestamps, duration, outcome, notes), call recordings where applicable, and appointments you schedule.
  • Billing information — subscription status, wallet balances, and transactions. Card/bank details are processed by Stripe and are not stored on our servers.
  • Google account data — if you connect Google Calendar and/or Google Ads, the data described in Section 4.
  • Usage and device data — log data, IP address, browser type, and marketing-attribution parameters.

3. How we use information

  • Operate the Service: authenticate you, route calls to eligible agents, and record outcomes.
  • Create and manage appointments, including syncing them to your Google Calendar when connected.
  • Show connected Google Ads campaign metrics alongside your call and revenue data (marketing attribution).
  • Process subscriptions, wallet top-ups, and marketplace purchases.
  • Send you transactional and account communications (see Section 5).
  • Provide reporting to your agency, secure the Service, and comply with the law.

4. Google user data

4a. Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional and is used solely to keep your scheduled appointments in sync. When you connect, you grant Inbound Select access to these scopes:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events

We use these scopes only to check your availability, create calendar events for appointments you schedule in Inbound Select, and update or remove those events when an appointment changes.

4b. Google Ads (read-only)

Agencies may optionally connect their own Google Ads account so that campaign spend can be shown beside call results. When an agency connects, it grants Inbound Select this scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords

What we access and why. We use this scope read-only to retrieve campaign-level reporting metrics (campaign name, status, impressions, clicks, cost, conversions) and display them alongside the agency’s own call and revenue data. We perform no write, mutate, or create operations — we never modify campaigns, budgets, keywords, bids, or ads. All Google Ads API calls originate from our backend; tokens are never exposed to the browser.

4c. Storage, Limited Use & revoking access

How we store it. We store the OAuth access and refresh tokens Google issues and the connected Google account identifier. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and scoped to the owning tenant, and are never placed in logs, client-side storage, or version control.

Limited Use. Inbound Select’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data (Calendar or Ads) for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it except to provide the Service (or with your consent, for security, or to comply with law), and do not use it to train generalized or third-party AI/ML models.

Revoking access. You can disconnect Google Calendar from Settings in Inbound Select, and an agency can disconnect Google Ads from the Marketing Hub, at any time — or from your Google Account permissions page. Disconnecting revokes our access and deletes the stored tokens.

5. Communications and email

We use a transactional email provider (Amazon SES) to send messages related to the Service, including: account invitations, one-time passcodes, invoices and receipts, subscription and billing notices, marketplace and call notifications, launchpad and support messages, and waitlist/early-access replies.

  • Basis. You receive these messages because you hold an account, were invited by an agency, or requested access. We do not send unrelated marketing email without consent.
  • Unsubscribe. You can opt out of non-essential email at any time using the unsubscribe link in those messages or by emailing us. Security- and billing-related messages (for example, one-time passcodes and invoices) are required to operate the Service and cannot be turned off while your account is active.
  • Bounces and complaints. We honor unsubscribe requests and automatically suppress addresses that hard-bounce or file a complaint, so we do not continue emailing them.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only: within your organization (agencies see their own agents’ activity); with service providers under contract — Stripe (payments and Stripe Connect, through which agencies collect their agents’ payments), Twilio (telephony and recordings), Google (calendar sync and, for agencies, read-only Ads reporting), Amazon Web Services (email delivery and secure file/recording storage), and our cloud host; for legal/safety reasons; or in a business transfer. Google user data is never sold and never shared for advertising.

7. Call recordings

Calls routed through the Service may be recorded and stored securely (Amazon S3) for quality, training, and reporting. Agencies and agents are responsible for providing any legally required notice to callers and for obtaining consent under the laws that apply to them (which may require one- or two-party consent depending on jurisdiction).

8. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations. Google OAuth tokens are deleted when you disconnect the relevant Google integration or close your account. Suppressed (unsubscribed/bounced) email addresses are retained only to honor those preferences.

9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, tenant isolation, and secure storage of credentials and OAuth tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Access, update, or correct your account information from your profile settings.
  • Disconnect Google Calendar or Google Ads at any time (Section 4).
  • Unsubscribe from non-essential email (Section 5).
  • Request a copy or deletion of your personal data by contacting us below.

11. Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 16.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time and will revise the “Last updated” date above.
